My story

Why I chose ecommerce bookkeeping

I started with general bookkeeping and accounting, learning how financial records come together and why accurate reporting matters.

As I learned more about ecommerce, I noticed something different about how these businesses operate.

An Amazon seller might make thousands in sales but receive a much smaller deposit after fees, refunds, and other adjustments. Shopify sellers have their own payout arrangements, and inventory adds another layer to the accounting.

Recording the bank deposit alone doesn't tell the full story.

That caught my attention.

I wanted to understand how these transactions move from the sales platforms into the books and how to reconcile them properly.

So I focused my training on ecommerce bookkeeping, QuickBooks Online, and A2X, and eventually built EcomKeeping around the work.

Today, that's still my focus: helping ecommerce businesses maintain accurate records and understand what their financial reports are telling them.

How I work

A practical approach to your books

  1. 1

    I understand your business first.

    Before recommending anything, I want to understand where your sales happen, how customers pay, which systems you use, and how everything currently gets recorded.

  2. 2

    I look beyond the bank deposits.

    A payout isn't the same as a sale. I pay attention to the sales, refunds, fees, transfers, and other transactions behind the money reaching your bank.

    I also look at inventory and cost of goods sold, because revenue alone doesn't tell you how profitable your business is.

  3. 3

    I keep things clear.

    You shouldn't have to be an accountant to understand your own reports.

    My goal is to give you organized financial records, explain important findings in plain English, and let you know when something needs attention.
