What I look at

A focused review of the areas that matter.

I'll tailor the checks to your sales channels and the information available. The review is meant to spot meaningful concerns, not investigate every transaction.

Financial statements

Unusual balances on your Balance Sheet and changes in sales, costs and margins across recent months.

Reconciliations and payouts

Whether banks, cards and relevant sales-channel clearing accounts are being reconciled, plus selected payout checks when source records are available.

Inventory and cost of goods sold

Whether the books appear to have a consistent approach to inventory and the costs of the products you've sold.

QuickBooks setup and classification

Account structure, unclassified transactions, fees, refunds, sales-tax liability bookkeeping and how relevant integrations are posting information.

What you receive

A short report with useful next steps.

I'll send a written summary covering the main observations, why they matter and what I recommend doing next. I'll distinguish items I could verify from those that need additional records or a more detailed diagnostic.

If nothing significant stands out within the checks performed, I'll tell you that too. A limited review cannot guarantee every issue will be found.
