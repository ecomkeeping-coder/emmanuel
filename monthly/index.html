What this service is

Not data entry. A complete monthly financial close.

An Amazon and Shopify seller has two payout structures, two fee architectures, a COGS layer that moves with every sale, and sales tax obligations that shift by state and platform. Most bookkeepers are not set up for that.

A2X pulls your full settlement data into QuickBooks Online with every fee, refund, and reserve correctly mapped. A net deposit never gets recorded as revenue.

Each month closes with an accurate P&L, Balance Sheet, and Cash Flow statement, plus a Loom video walking through what happened and what needs your attention.

Delivered by the 15th, or the next business day if the 15th falls on a weekend or holiday.

What every monthly close produces

  • Gross revenue correctly split across Amazon and Shopify
  • Every Amazon fee category separated and recorded
  • COGS tracked and inventory asset updated on the Balance Sheet
  • All bank accounts and clearing accounts reconciled to zero
  • Sales tax correctly classified as a liability, not income
  • Amazon reserve balance reconciled to your Seller Central account
  • P&L, Balance Sheet, and Cash Flow exported and delivered
  • KPI dashboard updated with real monthly figures
  • Loom video walkthrough of your numbers
  • Books coordinated with your CPA on request

Scope boundaries

What is not included in this service

The following need a different professional. I will point you to the right one.

  • Tax preparation and filing (your CPA or enrolled agent)
  • Sales tax return filing (a sales tax specialist or CPA)
  • Payroll processing (Gusto or ADP)
  • CFO services, forecasting, or financial modeling
  • General virtual assistant or admin tasks
  • PPC management, listing optimization, or product research
  • Legal or tax advice of any kind

A note on sales tax

Monitoring is included in every plan: tracking where you have economic nexus, flagging when you cross a threshold, and keeping tax collected in a liability account rather than income.


Filing and remitting is a licensed specialist's job, and I do not do it. What you and your CPA get is a clean Sales Tax Payable balance broken down by state.
