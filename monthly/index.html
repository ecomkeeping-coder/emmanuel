Why ecommerce bookkeeping needs special attention

Your sales, payouts and profits should tell the same story.

An Amazon settlement might include sales, refunds, referral fees, FBA charges and money held for a future payout.

Shopify can involve separate payment processors, discounts, refunds and sales-tax transactions.

If you also sell wholesale or through a physical store, there are more systems and payment flows to account for.

Simply recording the deposits that reach your bank doesn't give you a complete picture.

That's why my work goes beyond categorizing transactions. I reconcile the activity behind your sales, account for inventory costs and prepare reports that help you understand how your business is performing.

What every client receives

Bookkeeping handled properly, every month.

Every EcomKeeping monthly client receives the following services within their agreed scope.

Ecommerce sales and payout reconciliation

I account for sales, refunds, platform fees, payment processing charges, reserves and settlements, then reconcile the corresponding deposits.

Bank, credit card and clearing account reconciliation

Inventory valuation and cost of goods sold

I maintain inventory-value and COGS bookkeeping using an agreed accounting method and reliable product-cost and stock information.
