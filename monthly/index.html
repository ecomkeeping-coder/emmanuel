What this service is

Your sales are only part of the picture.

Between marketplace fees, refunds, supplier bills and stock purchases, it can be hard to see what is left. I bring those figures together so you can understand what your business is earning.

I use A2X or Link My Books to bring your sales data into QuickBooks Online, then reconcile your accounts and manage inventory and cost of goods sold. Your payout is matched to the sales, fees and refunds behind it.

You receive a P&L, Balance Sheet and Cash Flow statement, with a plain-English summary and a monthly call to talk through your results.

Reports are delivered by the 12th of the following month, with the records needed to close your books received by the 5th.

What every monthly close produces

  • Gross revenue correctly split across Amazon and Shopify
  • Every Amazon fee category separated and recorded
  • COGS tracked and inventory asset updated on the Balance Sheet
  • Bank, card and clearing accounts reconciled; any outstanding balances explained
  • Sales tax correctly classified as a liability, not income
  • Amazon reserve balance reconciled to your Seller Central account
  • P&L, Balance Sheet, and Cash Flow exported and delivered
  • Plain-English summary of your monthly results
  • Monthly call to discuss your reports
  • Year-end accountant coordination included in Growth
