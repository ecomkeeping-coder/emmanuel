The problem

A bank deposit is only part of the story.

Amazon and Shopify payouts can include fees, refunds and other adjustments. If your bookkeeping only follows the money arriving in the bank, it's easy to lose sight of what happened before the payout.

Sales and payouts don't line up.

You can see what landed in your account but not clearly trace it back to sales, refunds and fees.

Inventory costs are hard to follow.

Without reliable inventory and cost-of-goods-sold records, your gross profit can be misleading.

The month-end reports aren't helping.

Old unreconciled items and inconsistent coding make the numbers harder to explain and use.

My job is to connect those pieces, investigate differences and keep the books understandable.
